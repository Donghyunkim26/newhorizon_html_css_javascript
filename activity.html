A flexible approach

Activity is support for your whole wellbeing.

Movement can support your energy, mood, sleep, strength, and focus. It does not need to be intense or perfectly planned to be worthwhile.

The best activity is one that fits your body, your access, your interests, and the time you have today. Walking, stretching, dancing, gardening, sport, and active travel can all count.

Start with five minutes

Make it enjoyable

Choose music, a scenic route, a favourite sport, or an activity that helps you spend time with people you like.

Adapt the plan

Change the pace, length, location, or movement to suit your energy and ability. Consistency matters more than perfection.

Connection makes it easier

You can build momentum with other people.

Invite a friend for a walk, join a local class, practise with a family member, or ask someone to check in on your goal. Shared activity can make movement more welcoming and give you a reason to return.

Listen to your body

Make activity feel safe and sustainable.

  • Begin gently and build up gradually, especially after a break.
  • Drink water, wear suitable clothing, and choose a safe place to move.
  • Rest when you need to and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or unwell.
  • Ask a qualified health professional for personalised advice about an injury or health condition.
