A gentle practice

You can begin exactly where you are.

Mindfulness means noticing your thoughts, feelings, body, and surroundings without needing to fix everything immediately. A few attentive moments can help you respond instead of rushing on autopilot.

01

Notice

Pay attention to one thing you can see, hear, or feel.

02

Allow

Let the moment be here without judging yourself for it.

03

Return

When your mind wanders, gently bring it back to now.
