A kinder pause

Rest helps you return to what matters.

Stress can leave your body alert even after the difficult moment has passed. Relaxation gives your nervous system a chance to settle, which can support clearer thinking, steadier energy, sleep, and emotional wellbeing.

There is no single right way to relax. Choose something that feels safe, comfortable, and possible for you today.

A two-minute reset

Release tension one area at a time.

Move through the checklist slowly. You do not need to force a change; simply notice and soften where you can.

Small ways to make room for rest.

01

Change the pace

Take a slow walk, stretch gently, or make a warm drink without rushing the process.

02

Lower the input

Dim a light, put your phone away, or choose one quiet sound to focus on.

03

Protect a transition

Give yourself five minutes between work, study, responsibilities, and sleep.

Be gentle with yourself

Relaxation is support, not a test.

It is normal for your mind to wander or for relaxation to feel difficult at first. If stress, low mood, worry, or sleep problems are affecting everyday life, consider talking with a trusted person or qualified health professional.
