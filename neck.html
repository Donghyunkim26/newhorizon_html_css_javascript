A playful way to move

Jumping can add energy to your movement routine.

Small jumps can get your body moving and add variety to an activity break. You do not need special equipment or a perfect routine—start with a version that feels comfortable for you.

Jumping is not the right fit for everyone every day. Marching, heel raises, or stepping side to side are alternatives that keep you moving without leaving the floor.

Choose what feels realistic

Choose a jumping option

01

Try gentle jumps

Begin with a few small two-foot jumps, landing softly with knees relaxed. Pause between jumps whenever you need to.

02

Choose a lower-impact option

Step one foot out at a time instead of jumping, or try a march or heel raise if jumping does not feel comfortable today.

03

Build at your own pace

Start with a short set and rest between rounds. Increase the time or number of jumps gradually, only if it feels good.

Make it enjoyable

A little variety can make jumping more fun.

Put on a favourite song, follow a beginner-friendly routine, or invite someone to join you. Keep the movements simple and take turns choosing the next move so it stays relaxed and welcoming.

Jump to a songMove for one chorus, then pause or switch to steps.
Make a mini circuitAlternate a few jumps with marching or gentle stretching.
Celebrate the effortNotice how it feels to take part, rather than counting every jump.

Listen to your body

Set yourself up for a comfortable session.

  • Use a clear, non-slip surface and wear shoes that feel supportive.
  • Warm up with gentle marching and start with small, controlled movements.
  • Land softly, keep your knees relaxed, and take breaks when needed.
  • Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or unwell; seek personalised advice if you have an injury or health concern.

Your next step

Try a few comfortable moves today.

Start with a gentle warm-up, try a few small jumps or step-outs, then pause and check in with how your body feels. You can always choose the lower-impact option.
