Guided check-in

Reflection turns experience into understanding.

You do not need to analyse everything or find a perfect answer. A few honest questions can help you notice patterns, name your feelings, and recognise the small choices that support your wellbeing.

01

Be curious

Notice what happened without judging yourself for how you responded.

02

Be specific

Look for one feeling, need, or moment rather than solving everything at once.

03

Be kind

Speak to yourself with the same patience you would offer someone you care about.
